Citations
=========

Please cite racoon_clip and the relevant experimental modules used in your
analysis.

- Klostermann & Zarnack 2024:
  `racoon_clip—a complete pipeline for single-nucleotide analyses of iCLIP
  and eCLIP data <https://doi.org/10.1093/bioadv/vbae084>`_
- Verheyden & Klostermann *et al.* 2024:
  `A high-resolution map of functional miR-181 response elements in the
  thymus reveals the role of coding sequence targeting and an alternative
  seed match <https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkae416>`_
- Despic *et al.* 2026:
  `iCLIP3: A streamlined, non-radioactive protocol for mapping protein-RNA
  interactions in cellular transcripts at single-nucleotide resolution
  <https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.03.01.708747>`_
- Busch *et al.* 2020:
  `iCLIP data analysis: A complete pipeline from sequencing reads to RBP
  binding sites <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymeth.2019.11.008>`_

The command ``racoon_clip cite`` prints this citation information.
``racoon_clip citation`` remains available as an alias.
